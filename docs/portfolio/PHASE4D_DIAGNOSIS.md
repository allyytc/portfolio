# Phase 4D diagnosis and decisions

The two Phase 4D attachments are byte-identical. Work remains on local `main`; all original-site access has been read-only. The active Impact Studio HTML tab is unrelated editor context and is not a work target.

Largest observed gaps, based on actual Phase 4C source/browser renders:

1. **Window is present but reads as an unfinished side frame.** The small sky bay and outside-facing camera leave no strong recognizable architectural opening. Rebuild its reveal, jambs, sill, divided lights, latches and depth behind glazing; remove the weak frame instead of adding another flat overlay.
2. **Nook is not sufficiently recognizable.** Existing bench/cushion geometry is partly screened by the side frame. Keep licensed bench structure, give the window/bench a coherent silhouette, stage cream folded textiles and source-based upholstered cushions, and validate the default-camera view.
3. **Bedding is too slab-like and its fixed-light bake is defective.** Source renders are clean, but baked pillows contain black patches even after triangulation/normal correction. Replace the weak bed with the inspected KayKit model and shade soft furnishings separately using a color-only atlas and PBR lighting. Do not treat a clean source render as proof of a clean web export.
4. **Foliage repeats pointed leaves and simple cacti.** Replace weak repeated inserts with the previously audited Tiny Treats monstera, yucca and pothos silhouettes. The source review render confirms these are more detailed botanical models. Use a few varied placements, not all 100+ models.
5. **Furniture styles and material depth are uneven.** Keep the acceptable desk, ergonomic chair, printer and signs. Replace the angular lounge seat, preserve source gradient UVs, recolor blue upholstery to sage without tinting wood/white areas, and lower overly flat ambient light.
6. **Stair side geometry produces harsh striped artifacts.** Keep the working treads and circulation; replace only the malformed side strips with architectural timber stringers.
7. **Portfolio zones are present but partially screened.** Retain all 24 existing stable IDs, real owner media and reserved content. Make the sticker/sketch books and tablet readable in a distinct design area; no invented Unwrap UI or event photography.
8. **Composition still leaves excessive margins.** Keep the isometric orientation and named views, improve actual orthographic fit/canvas area, and check the full room for clipping. Do not apply a CSS transform to the model or add unrestricted orbit controls.

Phase 4C checkpoint retains source, GLB, camera/loader code, tests and rules. Existing completed architecture was exported and loaded correctly; the main problem is asset silhouette/shading/composition, not a cached missing room. Source and GLB stable IDs were inspected. New source permissions are recorded before integration in `PHASE4D_ASSETS.md`; paid sources and unlicensed personal portfolio assets are excluded.
