# Phase 7C visual audit

Verified against the actual 1600px browser capture `evidence/phase7c/before-desktop.png` and live Blender `PortfolioLoft4D`, not just older reports.

1. The stage is a document section: header, metadata, footer and Quick View consume separate layout rows. Beige margins dominate.
2. A transparent canvas and no exterior context make the loft read as a product illustration rather than a place.
3. The daylight-baked interior is isolated against an almost identical background; no warm/cool environmental depth.
4. Architectural cutaway has no roof silhouette, exterior boards, attached porch or entry sequence.
5. Design is an inconspicuous desk screen; no recognizable art station.
6. The right nook has a real framed window and bench (not missing), but lacks a hearth, fire and visual anchor.
7. The navigation logic is robust, but the large inline Quick View and standard modal treatment are disconnected from the objects.
8. Existing furniture and plants are licensed modeled assets, not disposable placeholders; replacing them wholesale would lose quality and functioning anchors.
